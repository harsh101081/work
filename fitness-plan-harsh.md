## 3. The Protein Truth (read this once)

Hitting **140–150g protein on pure veg + dairy with NO whey, at only 1,930 kcal**, is the hardest part of your plan. Here's why and how we solve it:

- Veg protein foods (dal, rajma, chana) come with ~3x their protein in carbs — eat enough to hit 145g protein that way and you blow past your calorie limit.
- **The fix: soya chunks are your hero food.** 100g dry soya = **~52g protein for only ~345 kcal** at ~₹14. Nothing else veg comes close.
- Realistic landing zone on this plan: **135–145g protein**. Each day below uses **80–100g of dry soya + dairy + paneer/tofu** to get there. That's **~1.7 g/kg bodyweight — scientifically plenty** for muscle retention in a deficit (benefits plateau around this point).
- **If you ever spare ₹/month for even a 1kg whey tub:** one scoop = 24g protein for ~₹40, and hitting 150g becomes effortless. Until then, soya does the job.

> Each day targets **~1,930 kcal / ~140g protein**. Numbers are approximate (±10%) — eyeball portions, don't weigh obsessively.

## 13. Progressive Overload in a Deficit

In a calorie deficit your job is **mostly to MAINTAIN strength** (that's what tells your body to keep the muscle). Don't expect big PRs — flat or slightly up is a win.

- **Double progression:** pick a rep range (e.g., 6–8). Hit the top of the range on all sets → next session add the smallest weight increment (2.5kg barbell / next dumbbell). Drop back to bottom of range and build up again.
- **Track the big 4:** Bench, Row/Pulldown, Overhead Press, Squat. If these hold steady through the cut, you're keeping your muscle.
- **Reps-in-reserve check:** if a weight that was RPE 8 becomes RPE 9–10 for the same reps, that's fatigue from the deficit — hold the weight, don't force progression.
- **Realistic benchmark:** over 8–12 weeks expect to *maintain* (±5%) your main lifts and possibly add a little to smaller lifts. Newer trainees at higher body fat (you) may even gain some — bonus, not the expectation.

## 14. Training Intensity, Recovery & Overtraining

**How hard to push while cutting:**
- Compounds: hard but clean — RPE 8, leave 2 reps. Don't grind ugly reps; injury risk rises when fatigued + under-fuelled.
- Isolations: take the last set to RPE 9–10, it's safe and effective.
- **Reduce VOLUME before INTENSITY.** If you're run down, cut 1–2 sets per exercise rather than dropping the weight or skipping — this keeps the muscle-retention signal while easing recovery load.

**Sleep (non-negotiable for fat loss + muscle + abs):**
- **Target 7–9 h/night (aim 8).** Poor sleep raises cortisol, increases hunger, and is the #1 reason cuts stall and muscle is lost.
- Keep your 2nd tea/coffee before ~5 PM.

**Active recovery (rest days):** light 30–45 min walk, gentle stretching/mobility, foam rolling. Don't sit fully sedentary — it helps recovery and adds steps.

**Deload:** every **6–8 weeks**, or sooner if beat up — take 1 week at ~50% sets and ~10% lighter loads. You'll often come back *stronger*. This is part of the plan, not a setback.

**Overtraining / under-recovery warning signs:**
- Strength dropping 2+ sessions in a row
- Persistent fatigue, poor/restless sleep, elevated resting heart rate
- Constant soreness, achy joints, nagging niggles
- Low mood/irritability, no motivation to train
- Getting sick often, appetite swings (ravenous or none)

→ If you see these: take 2–3 extra rest days, bump calories to maintenance for a few days, prioritise sleep, then resume. **Pushing through these is how you lose muscle and get injured.**

**Signs you're under-eating too aggressively:** strength tanking, always cold, dizzy, brain fog, libido drop, fat loss stalls *despite* compliance. Fix = add ~150 kcal carbs and/or take a maintenance-calorie "diet break" week.

## 17. Form Cues & Safety (key lifts)

- **Bench press:** shoulder blades pinched + down, slight arch, feet planted, bar to lower chest, elbows ~45°, full lockout. Always use a spotter or safety pins for heavy sets.
- **Row:** hinge ~45°, neutral spine (no rounding), pull to lower ribs, squeeze shoulder blades, don't yank with momentum.
- **Overhead press:** brace core + glutes, bar over mid-foot, don't lean back excessively, full lockout overhead.
- **Squat:** brace before descending, knees track over toes, hit at least parallel, drive through mid-foot, chest up. Use safety pins.
- **Romanian deadlift:** soft knees, push hips *back*, bar stays close to legs, feel the hamstring stretch, neutral spine — stop before your back rounds.
- **General:** brace your core on every heavy rep, control the lowering (don't drop the weight), full range of motion > ego weight. **A tweak from sloppy form in a deficit can derail weeks of progress.**

## 19. Synergy Tips (how it all works together)

- **Meal timing → performance:** lunch (1–2 PM) fuels the session; the light banana + coffee pre-workout tops up quick energy without sitting heavy in your stomach during squats. Heavy/oily food before 2:30 = sluggish session — keep pre-workout light.
- **Post-workout window:** get 25–35g protein + carbs within ~45 min of finishing — this is when soya/paneer + rice does the most for recovery and muscle retention. Not magic, but a solid habit.
- **Caffeine = free pre-workout:** black coffee 20–30 min before the gym gives you the afternoon energy lift for ₹5–8. Keep all caffeine before ~5 PM so it doesn't wreck the sleep that's driving your fat loss.
- **Creatine + cut:** if/when you add it, take 5g daily *consistently* — timing doesn't matter. The small water-weight bump (1–2 kg) is intramuscular water (good, looks fuller), not fat.
- **Hydration around training:** dehydration kills strength fast. Front-load water before the session, sip during, rehydrate after — especially on Mumbai-hot leg days (add salt + lemon).
- **Steps are your fat-loss accelerator:** since you don't do cardio, the 10–12k daily steps quietly create extra deficit with zero impact on lifting recovery. This is the single easiest lever to speed up the abs timeline.
